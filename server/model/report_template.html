      Anomaly detection

      When a flow stops being normal

      A flow is unusual when it runs longer, uses more or runs faster than any normal flow seen at that hour (±1 h) since 2024, times one shared margin set on 2025 for about one false alarm a month. Floors: 3 min and 20 L. On the device the duration limit also stays under 3/4 of the Tier 1 shut-off, so the notification comes first.

      Evaluation

      Synthetic leaks in the test period

      • Short flows at shower rates (3-12 L/min for 5 min) look like a shower and mostly pass. A leak that keeps going is stopped by the device's own Tier 1 limit, which does not depend on this model.
      • The Mahalanobis score (one robust 2-D Gaussian of duration and volume per hour) at the same false-alarm budget barely detects anything: a long 12 L/min flow sits on the same line as a normal one.
